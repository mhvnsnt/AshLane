# Urban Reign Mission Analysis (Real Research 2026-10-05)
Source: GameFAQs walkthrough, missions 1-45 analyzed.

## Mission Archetypes (with concrete examples)

### 1. EXTERMINATE ("Take everyone down")
- Most common. Missions 1, 2, 7, 8, 9, 12, 13, 17, 19, 25, 27, 29, 31, 33, 43, 44.
- Example M17 "Crashing the Party": 5 opponents at once.
- Example M25 "Outnumbered": 5v1.

### 2. ASSASSINATE ("Take [specific] down")
- Target one enemy, ignore others.
- M3 "Declaration": Take Busta down (1v1).
- M10 "Observer": Take Glen's MEN down, DON'T touch Glen (he'll destroy you).
- M14 "Right Stuff": Take Glen down, avoid Jake.
- M34 "The Agitator": Take Bain down, ignore Zaps.
- M45 "The Duel": Take Chris down, Outlaws won't attack unless provoked.

### 3. CHOICE ("Take one of them down")
- M4 "Friendly Gesture": 3 opponents, take any ONE.
- M5 "Child's Play": 3 opponents, take any ONE.

### 4. TIMED ("within Time Limit")
- M6 "Turf": 2 minutes to beat Reggie.
- M18 "Hit-and-Run": Time limit vs. Rod/Torque.
- M38 "The Derelict": Time limit.

### 5. BODY REGION ("Bust up [region]")
- M23 "Avenging the Honor": Bust up Zach's LOWER body (sweep kicks, lower grapples).
- This is the regional damage system — not just HP.

### 6. ESCORT ("Protect [partner]")
- M36 "Despicable Ally": Protect Glen Kluger.
- M40 "Dead Run": Protect Dwayne Davis (he's already fighting when you arrive).
- M41 "Traitor": Protect Grimm.
- M42 "Lucky Error": Protect Tong Yoon.

### 7. WEAPON
- M28 "Who's Your Daddy?": Glen has a crowbar. Knock it out of his hand, pick it up.

### 8. BOSS (1v1, high stats)
- M11 "The Proof": Jake Hudson in bar (use tables/chairs).
- M15 "Fury": Kadonashi (don't try to out-punch him, use deflections).
- M20 "The Hungry Wolf": Tong Yoon in warehouse (throw into shelves).
- M30 "Showdown": Dwayne Davis (end of Chapter 1).

## Key Mechanics That Make It Feel Real

1. **Environmental damage**: Throw opponents into tables, chairs, shelves, walls for extra damage. (M11, M14, M20, M24)
2. **Partner system**: M31+ introduces partners. Double Team attacks. R2 commands.
3. **Regional damage**: Head/Upper/Lower body have separate health. M23 requires lower body destruction.
4. **Weapon persistence**: Enemies HAVE weapons (M28). You can disarm and use them.
5. **Enemy AI variety**: Some deflect often (Jose, Miguel), some grapple (Jake), some use SPA (Chris). Not all the same.
6. **Stat upgrades**: 1-3 points per mission. Spend on body regions.

## What AshLane Should Adapt (Not Copy)

1. **Win conditions beyond "kill all"**: Assassinate, escort, timed, region-specific.
2. **Environmental kills**: Throw into hazards for bonus damage.
3. **Partner commands**: Simple R2 menu (help, double-team, etc.)
4. **Enemy archetypes**: Not all enemies fight the same. Grapplers, strikers, deflectors.
5. **Progression**: Points after mission → upgrade body regions.
