## 1. TEKKEN 7 / TEKKEN 8

### Tekken 7 main menu (single horizontal row of tiles)
Story: The Mishima Saga · Arcade · Treasure Battle · Versus · Online · Practice · Customize · Gallery · Options
(Jukebox = PS-only main-menu item.)

### Tekken 8 main menu (tabbed Offline/Online + utility tiles)
**Offline**: Story: The Dark Awakens (15 chapters) · Character Episodes (32 chars × 5 fights each) · Arcade Quest (avatar campaign, 6 chapters) · Arcade Battle (8-opponent ladder) · VS (PvP / Player vs CPU / CPU vs CPU) · Practice · Super Ghost Battle (AI ghosts trained on playstyles) · Tekken Ball (volleyball minigame)
**Online**: Ranked / Quick / Player / Group matches · Tekken Fight Lounge (100-player social lobby: Battle Area, Customization Shop, Tekken Dojo, Beach Area) · Practice · Tekken Ball — full cross-play, "Match Anywhere" queues
**Utility**: Customization · My Replays & Tips · Gallery · Jukebox (all platforms — music from every Tekken game, per-stage assignment) · Options

### Character select
Portrait-card grid, P1 left / P2 right, random-select tile. Costume presets picked after character; T8 custom outfits live in slots (first 4 visible, scroll for more). T7 shows name only (no style/difficulty labels).

### Story / Arcade
T7: Mishima Saga = 13 chapters + prologue/epilogue + Special Chapter (Shin Akuma); ~28 single-fight Character Episodes. Arcade ladder ends at Devil Kazumi.
T8: 15-chapter campaign; Character Episodes for all 32 (5 fights + still-art endings each).

### Tournament
T7: online brackets up to 8 players, single/double elimination. T8: "Tournament settings" toggle forces standard rules; Group Match + Fight Lounge fill the social role.

### Practice (the training-mode gold standard)
T7: Player/Opponent settings, 5 recordable CPU actions, movelist, sample combos, Display settings (input history, damage, recovery-frame highlight); frame data = paid DLC13.
T8: Combo Challenges (11/char) · Punishment Training · Dummy defense settings + record · free live frame data (startup + advantage) · stage select. **My Replays & Tips: take control inside a replay for ~10 seconds to practice alternate options** — the killer feature.

### Customization
T7: **Character** (hair, upper/lower clothes, accessories with reposition/resize/rotate, face paint, auras — all Fight-Money-bought, cosmetic only) + **Player** (health bars, panels, titles).
T8: **Character** (new separate Shoes slot, 2 accessory slots, face subcategories: eyes/makeup/brows/cheeks/lips — but can't mix base-outfit pieces with customs) + **Avatar** (Arcade Quest/Lounge avatar: face/skin/hair/motion/outfits) + **Player** (bars, backgrounds, titles, Magic Mirror toggle).

### Jukebox
T7: PS-only; tracks Tekken 1→TTT2 + T7; 3 custom playlists; per-stage assignment incl. Round 1 / Final Round music.
T8: all platforms; tracks from every Tekken game; per-stage assignment.

### Options
T7: Game Options (difficulty Very Easy→Ultra Hard, rounds, timer, vibration, Story Assist) · Screen · Sound · Controller Setup (per-player remapping).
T8: Game Option Settings (rounds VS/Ghost/Tekken Ball, round duration, vibration, rollback, tournament settings, cross-play) · Controller Setup (full button map incl. Heat/Rage/Special Style) · Sound (master/BGM/SFX/voice/environmental, subtitles) · Screen (HUD position, menu character display) · Avatar Camera · Graphics (PC) · Color Support.

## 2. WWE 2K24 / 2K25

### Main menu (2K24)
Play · Showcase · Universe · MyGM · MyFACTION · MyRISE · Creations · Online · Extras
(2K25 adds **The Island** — a persistent online hub, next-gen only.)

### Play (exhibition) flow — the gold standard for match setup
1. **Format select**: One on One · Two on Two · Triple Threat · Fatal 4-Way · 5/6/8-Superstar · Gauntlet · Royal Rumble · WarGames · Handicap · Tournament
2. **Stipulation select** (~80 combos): Normal · Extreme Rules · TLC · Hell in a Cell · Steel Cage · Last Man Standing · Iron Man · Falls Count Anywhere · Backstage Brawl · Ambulance · Casket · Special Guest Referee · Ladder · Table · Submission · (2K25: Bloodline Rules, Underground)
3. **Participant select**: roster grid, assign each slot to controller or AI, alternate attires, managers
4. **Arena select**: categorized (current weekly/PLE, classic, WrestleMania legacy III–40, MyRise, custom, DLC)
5. **Rules tabs**: Win Conditions (Pin/Submission/KO/Finisher Match/First Blood/cage escapes) · DQ on/off · Rope Break · Ring-Out count · Elimination · Falls Count Anywhere · Time Limit · Entrance Interval · Interference (entrance/mid/post run-ins) · Match Settings (finisher stocks, default health, cage/chamber/cell types) · Custom Weapons Wheel (6 slots)
6. **Accept** → Entrances on/off → match

### Creation Suite (13 modes)
Custom Superstar · Referee · Championship · Entrance (Template/Motion/Title Motion/MITB Motion/Light Show/Screen Effect/Music/Titantron+Banner+Stage&Ramp+Apron&Ringpost+Barricade movies) · Victory · Moveset (attacks/grapples/ground/running/diving/turnbuckle/weapon + Signatures/Finishers/Super Finishers/taunts/Paybacks) · Arena · Show · Money in the Bank · Video · Image · Custom Matches (saved rule sets) · Community Creations (upload/download)

CAS tabs: Preset Templates · Face · Body · Hair & Facial · Clothing Head/Upper/Lower · Superstar Information (name/nickname/hometown/gender/reaction/weight class/commentary name, Alternate Attire tab, referee attire)

### Options (Extras → Options)
- **Gameplay**: Difficulty (Easy/Normal/Hard/Legend) · Weight Detection · Stamina · Submission/Pin mini-game types · Trading Blows · Chain Wrestling · Run-ins (entrance/mid/post) · Blood · Subtitles · Tutorial Tips
- **AI sliders**: reversal rates (strike/grapple/ground/finisher/weapon) · behavior rates (combo/submission/dive/grapple/strike/dodge) · reversal windows · stamina/recovery · damage scaling
- **Presentation**: camera (Standard/Third-Person) · reversal prompt · camera cuts · Super Finisher VFX
- **Audio**: Jukebox (per-song toggles) · commentary/effects volume
- **Controller**: fully remappable

### Other modes
- **Universe**: sandbox booking — shows/PLE calendar, rosters, rivalries (type + Low→Very High intensity, Rivalry Actions: run-ins/cash-ins/brawls), championships (double-title), match table editing, simulate-or-play
- **MyRISE**: story campaign (2K24: two stories, 80 rewards; 2K25 "Mutiny": unified, 2 custom superstars, 3 personalities, 9 chapters)
- **MyGM**: GM pick (unique Power Cards) → brand pick → draft → weekly loop (book matches, rivalries, promos, trades) → Hall of Fame
- **Showcase**: match list with mid-match objectives recreating iconic moments
- **Extras**: Roster viewer · Jukebox · Controls · Performance Center (tutorials) · Credits · Store

## 3. DEF JAM: FIGHT FOR NY (2004)

### Main menu (7 items — no online on any platform)
Story Mode · Battle Mode · Unlock Rewards · Beat Box (jukebox) · Options · Extras · High Scores
(Up to 8 User IDs per memory card; separate Options File vs User ID file save model.)

### Story mode — the crib hub
- **Message Center (Sidekick)**: plot messages/fight invitations — this is how fights trigger
- **5-borough NYC map**: travel to clubs, gym, Staten Island shops
- **Trophy Room**: milestone tracking
- **Wardrobe** (+ mirror preview; Charisma decays if you don't change clothes)
- **Beat Box**: customize soundtrack playlist
- **5 shops**: Stapleton Athletics (gym — Henry Rollins: Development Points → Upper/Lower/Toughness/Health/Speed stats + Blazin' moves (4 slots) + 2 extra styles) · Jacob & Co. (jewelry → Charisma) · Stingray's Barbershop (hair/facial hair/color) · The Syndicate (clothes — licensed lines, wear-style options) · Manny's Tats (tattoos → Charisma)

### Character creation ("police sketch" framing)
Face shape → body type (Puffy→Biggie) → skin/eyes/brows/nose/mouth/ears (~8 each) → hair/color/facial hair → voice (6 options) → 1 of 5 fighting styles:
Street (Haymaker KO) · Kickboxing (kick-combo KO) · Martial Arts (wall-attack KO) · Wrestling (grapple KO) · Submissions (submission KO)

### Battle mode flow
Match type → Ruleset → Fighters (up to 4 humans; CPU/human assignment) → Venue
**9 match types**: One on One · Team (2v2, both must fall) · Free For All · Cage · Ring Out (breakable barriers) · Inferno (fire ring + falling debris) · Demolition (total opponent's SUV) · Subway (train = instant KO) · Window (4-story drop)
**3 rulesets**: Standard · Crowd Favorite (points-based — winner needn't score the KO) · No Submissions
**24 venues**, each with unlock requirement and unique trait (train KO, window fatality, fire, car-totaling, breakable pillars, circuit-breaker shock…)

### Options
Difficulty · Vibration · Autosave · Audio · User ID management (create/delete/copy) · Autoload/memory-card

### Extras
Unlock Rewards (spend reward points) · Cheats (21 codes) · Beat Box jukebox · High Scores · Soul Plane trailer · stage fatalities
Post-match scoring: ~20 bonus categories (Combos, Blazin' Moves, World Warrior, Perfect Match…) + penalties (Coward, Soft Touch, First To Fall, Recycled Re-Run)

## 4. URBAN REIGN (PS2, 2005)

### Main menu
NEW GAME · CONTINUE · FREE · MULTIPLAYER · CHALLENGE (unlocks after story) · EXTRAS · OPTIONS
(Cheat codes entered at title screen; exclamation-mark confirmation.)

### Story mode
- NEW GAME → always start as Brad Hawk, Mission 1. Difficulty is global (OPTIONS → DIFFICULTY, 5 levels: Very Easy→Very Hard).
- **Mission briefing screen**: stage photo, mission number + name, opponent list, objective. Fail → Retry / back to briefing / Quit.
- **100 missions, 3 chapters** (1–30, 31–70, 71–100). Objectives: exterminate, assassinate, timed, region-break (bust up a body region), escort/protect partner.
- **Partners from Chapter 2**: (No Partner) / (Partner Allowed) / fixed (Partner: X). R2 issues commands (aid / double-team / hand weapon); can switch control to partner; Brad dying ends mission.
- **After EVERY mission: upgrade screen, 1–3 skill points** → STK (Strike) · GRP (Grapple) · RGA (Regional Attack) · SPA (Special Attack) · WPA (Weapon Attack) · TGH (Toughness) · HDE/UBE/LBE (Head/Upper/Lower Endurance). Milestones unlock new moves (running power bomb, air grabs, directional SPAs).

### Character select (Free/Multiplayer/Challenge)
Portrait grid, name + fighting style shown (All-Round, Kung Fu, Power, Karate, Boxing, Rush, Wrestling, Muay Thai, Capoeira, Tae Kwon Do, Street, Submission…). Only unlocked fighters; same-character blocked by default; **no costume select**; maxed stats after story completion.

### Free Mode
Pick chapter → numbered mission list. Play as any unlocked non-opponent fighter. Partner only where allowed. **E→S letter-rank results screen** scored on: Clear Time · Max Combo · Damage Taken · Partner KO · Recovery Item use · Play Bonus · Fighter Points (lower-tier fighters = higher bonus). Rank-gated character unlocks (10 characters).

### Multiplayer (up to 4, multitap; AI fills slots)
- **Versus**: Knock Out · Damage Amount · Regional KO on/off · Double Team KO on/off · Quick SPA Gauge on/off
- **Weapon Battle** (one weapon mid-arena): Weapon Scramble (hold at timeout) · Continuous Possession · Total Possession Time · KO Decision on/off · Max Weapon Damage on/off · SPA-0 on/off
- **Destruction Battle** (smash enemy's totem with mallets): Weapon / Knockdown / Demolition KO on/off / Quick SPA Gauge
- Common: 1v1 · 2v2 · 3/4-fighter battle royale · Stage · 1–3 Rounds · 1/3/5-min Time · Music (Stage/Random)

### Challenge Mode
Wave survival (up to 2 enemies/match), health regenerates between matches, SPA starts at half, no time limit. 10–50 battles, difficulty, enemy stats, music, Single/Team, 1–2 players. Unlocks Paul Phoenix.

### Fighter Files (Extras)
Unlocked-character profiles only: style, height/weight, DOB, bio, stat block. No in-game move lists.

### Tutorial / Practice (Extras)
Tutorial = command reference list (not structured lessons). Practice = unlimited health/time vs chosen characters; START → QUIT to exit.

### Options
GAME (camera, target marker) · CONTROLLER (vibration, configs) · DISPLAY (position, brightness, 4:3/16:9) · SOUND (MUSIC/VOICE/SE volumes) · DIFFICULTY (5 levels) · AUTOSAVE · SAVE PROFILE.

### Extras
Tutorial · Practice · Fighter Files · Credits. **No movie gallery, art gallery, or sound test.**

## 5. SYNTHESIS — What AshLane's Menu System Needs

The superset of all four games, adapted for a Yakuza-style open-world street brawler. Ordered by priority.

### TIER 1 — Core game flow (AshLane is missing all of this)

**5.1 Main menu (real hierarchy, not flat buttons)**
```
ASHLANE
├── STORY (open-world campaign — the Yakuza loop)
├── EXHIBITION (Def Jam Battle Mode / WWE Play flow)
│   ├── Match Type → Rules → Fighters → Venue → Accept
├── FREE ROAM (explore the district, no mission)
├── MULTIPLAYER (local 2P now; online later)
├── CHALLENGE (wave survival — Urban Reign rules)
├── CUSTOMIZE (creation suite — see 5.4)
├── JUKEBOX (Def Jam Beat Box / Tekken Jukebox)
├── FIGHTER FILES (Urban Reign — bios, stats, unlock conditions)
├── OPTIONS
└── EXTRAS (gallery, credits)
```
*Sources: Tekken's tabbed Offline/Online split; WWE's Play→format→stipulation→participants→arena→rules chain; Def Jam's 7-item main menu; Urban Reign's NEW/CONTINUE/FREE/MULTIPLAYER/CHALLENGE split.*

**5.2 Exhibition setup flow (copy WWE's chain, Def Jam's content)**
1. **Match type select** — the union of Def Jam's 9 and Urban Reign's multiplayer modes, adapted:
   One on One · Tag Team (2v2) · Free For All (3–4) · Cage · Ring Out (breakable barriers) · Inferno (fire) · Demolition (wreck the car) · Subway (train KO) · Window Drop · Weapon Scramble (Urban Reign) · Destruction (totem-smash)
2. **Rules select** — per type, like WWE's rules tabs: win condition (KO / points / ring-out / region-break) · time limit · DQ on/off · weapon spawns · rounds
3. **Fighter select** — portrait grid (Tekken/Urban Reign), P1 left / P2 right, name + style shown, random tile, CPU/human assignment per slot (Def Jam/WWE)
4. **Venue select** — categorized list with unlock requirements and unique-trait labels (Def Jam's 24-venue model: each venue lists its hazard)
5. **Accept → fight**

**5.3 Character select screen spec**
- Portrait-card grid (all four games do this — stop using text lists)
- Per card: name, fighting style, difficulty tier
- P1/P2 sides, random-select tile
- Costume/attire picker after character (Tekken presets; WWE alternate attires)
- CPU-fill for empty slots (Urban Reign/WWE)
- Locked characters shown silhouetted with unlock hint (Fighter Files cross-link)

### TIER 2 — Progression and identity

**5.4 Customization / creation suite (the Def Jam crib + WWE CAS + Tekken slots)**
- **Fighter creation**: face/body/hair/facial hair/voice (Def Jam's police-sketch flow, ~8 options per facial feature) + body-type slider
- **Attire tabs** (WWE CAS structure): Head · Upper · Lower · Shoes (Tekken 8 split shoes out — do the same) · Accessories (2 slots, repositionable like Tekken) · Tattoos · Face paint
- **Fighting style pick**: 3–4 AshLane styles with unique KO conditions (Def Jam's 5 styles — each style needs a signature finisher, not just a damage bonus)
- **Moveset editor** (WWE lite): signatures, finishers, taunts per style
- **Gym/training** (Def Jam Stapleton): spend Rep points → stats (Upper/Lower/Toughness/Health/Speed) + buy Heat moves + learn extra styles
- **Shops** (Def Jam's 5-shop model, reskinned): clothes shop (Charisma/Heat gain), jewelry, barbershop, tattoo parlor — gear must be mechanical (Heat gain), not cosmetic
- **Jukebox tie-in**: Beat Box in the safehouse — playlist of unlocked tracks, per-venue assignment (Tekken)

**5.5 Story-mode hub (Def Jam crib + Yakuza safehouse)**
- Message center (phone — missions arrive as messages/calls, GTA-style)
- District map (travel to mission markers — never teleport the player)
- Trophy/achievement wall
- Wardrobe with mirror preview (Charisma decays if you don't change clothes — steal this)
- Between-mission upgrade screen (Urban Reign: 1–3 points per mission → regional stats)

**5.6 Fighter Files**
Unlocked-character bios: style, height/weight, record, stat block, unlock condition, move list. (Urban Reign has bios but no move lists — AshLane should include move lists, beating the reference.)

### TIER 3 — Training, replay, polish

**5.7 Practice mode (Tekken's gold standard, minimum viable)**
- Dummy settings (stand/block/record actions), stage select, both costumes selectable
- Display toggles: input history, damage numbers, frame advantage
- Combo challenges per character; punishment training
- *Stretch goal*: Tekken 8's take-control-inside-replay

**5.8 Challenge mode (Urban Reign rules)**
Wave survival, health regen between waves, 10–50 battles, Single/Team, 1–2 players, E→S letter ranks (Urban Reign's 7-category scoring: time, max combo, damage taken, partner KO, item use, play bonus, fighter points)

**5.9 Jukebox**
- Unlocked-track library (songs unlock via story/cheats — Def Jam's 21 cheat-code songs)
- Custom playlists, per-venue assignment (Tekken), menu music assignment
- Lives in safehouse AND main menu (both games do this)

**5.10 Options (superset of all four)**
- Gameplay: difficulty (5 levels like Urban Reign), rounds, round timer, vibration, assists
- AI sliders (WWE's inventory): reversal rates, aggression, reversal windows
- Display: brightness, HUD position, aspect
- Sound: master/BGM/SFX/voice sliders + jukebox toggles
- Controller: fully remappable (Tekken/WWE), per-player
- Camera: lock-on marker, camera cuts on/off (WWE/Urban Reign)
- Save: autosave, profile management, multiple slots

**5.11 Extras**
Gallery (movies/art — Tekken), credits, cheats entry (Def Jam's 21 codes; Urban Reign's title-screen codes), controls reference, tutorials (WWE Performance Center model: structured lessons, not Urban Reign's bare command list)

### What NOT to copy
- WWE's wrestling-specific stipulations (Royal Rumble, MITB, WarGames) — brawler doesn't need them
- Tekken's Fight Lounge / online infrastructure — later phase
- Def Jam's licensed music/artists — original content only
- Urban Reign's lack of costume select and missing move lists — beat the reference, don't repeat its gaps
- Any game's bare-bones tutorial — WWE Performance Center / Tekken punishment training is the bar

### Implementation order (recommended)
1. Main menu hierarchy + exhibition setup chain (5.1, 5.2) — unblocks everything
2. Character select screen (5.3) — replaces text lists
3. Options menu (5.10) — cheap, high value
4. Jukebox (5.9) — cheap, high player delight
5. Customization suite (5.4) — the big build
6. Story hub (5.5) + Fighter Files (5.6)
7. Practice (5.7) + Challenge (5.8) + Extras (5.11)
