# The Dynasty Authority — Visual Design Bible

**Faction:** The Dynasty Authority (police faction)
**AshLane display name:** "The Dynasty Authority"
**Motto:** "Peace is mandatory."
**Design principle:** Uniforms MATCH (same faction, police blues/tactical) but are INDIVIDUAL (different pieces per personality). Nobody looks like a clone.

**Owner spec source:** 2026-10-05 voice notes. Based-on wrestlers confirmed by owner. Do NOT change without owner approval.

---

## 1. CAPTAIN SILAS "The System"

**Role:** Commissioner — faction leader, Level 5 Boss
**Based on:** Silas Young (ROH wrestler)

### Picture references (for artists/modelers)
- Silas Young: "The Last Real Man" gimmick
- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silas_Young
- ROH profile photos — search "Silas Young ROH 2018"

### Physical reference (Silas Young)
- White man, 5'11", ~220 lb, stocky/powerful build
- **Slicked-back hair** (signature)
- **Thick mustache** (signature — "Last Real Man" look)
- Hard-edged face, rancorous eyes
- Blue-collar toughness, old-school

### AshLane uniform
- **White collar shirt** — top buttons OPEN, sleeves ROLLED UP
- Gun belt with holstered sidearm, cuffs, radio
- Dark slacks (charcoal/navy)
- Polished boots
- Badge on belt, not chest (he doesn't need to show it)
- Optional: reading glasses on a chain, tablet in hand

### Vibe
Cold bureaucrat. Treats people as variables. Reviews your "data" before the raid. Never raises his voice — the mustache does the intimidating for him.

### Fighting style
Technical/balanced. Reads patterns, punishes mistakes. (AshLane: `martial-arts`, archetype `balanced`)

## 2. "BIG DAWG" TITUS

**Role:** S.W.A.T. breacher — muscle enforcer, Level 4
**Based on:** Titus O'Neil (WWE)

### Picture references (for artists/modelers)
- Titus O'Neil: WWE profile
- WWE.com: https://www.wwe.com/superstars/titus-oneil
- Search "Titus O'Neil Prime Time Players"

### Physical reference (Titus O'Neil)
- Black man, **6'6", 270 lb** — the biggest on the team
- **Bald**, brown eyes
- Sculpted/powerhouse physique (former football defensive end)
- Broad shoulders, thick arms
- Charismatic even when silent

### AshLane uniform
- **Full riot gear** — chest plate, shoulder pads, forearm guards, shin guards
- **NO riot helmet** (face visible — he's not hiding)

- "POLICE" markings on chest plate

- Optional: battering ram or breaching tool on back

### Vibe
First through the door, last one standing. Big, loud, loves the gear. The battering ram of the Dynasty. Been thrown off a roof twice. Got back up twice.

### Fighting style
Powerhouse brawler. Overwhelms with size. (AshLane: `street`, archetype `bruiser`)

## 4. FINN "THE PRIEST" MAC

**Role:** "The Chaplain" — spiritual enforcer, Level 4
**Based on:** Finn Balor / Prince Devitt

### Picture references (for artists/modelers)
- Finn Balor: WWE "Demon" and regular looks
- Prince Devitt: NJPW Bullet Club era
- Search "Finn Balor WWE" for current look

### Physical reference (Finn Balor)
- White man (Irish), ~5'11", ~190 lb — lean/athletic
- Dark hair, often slicked or textured
- Lean muscular build (not bulky — agile striker)
- Intense eyes

### AshLane uniform
- **Priest/chaplain outfit** — NOT standard police blue
- **Black clerical shirt with white priest collar** (the signature piece)
- Dark tactical pants (not slacks — he moves)
- Gun belt worn OVER the clerical shirt (the contradiction is the point)
- Nightstick held/carried **like a crucifix**
- Cross patch or pin on shoulder
- Tactical boots

### Vibe
Religious fanatic. Genuinely believes the badge is divine mandate. Demands confessions mid-fight: "Confess! Resistance is a sin against the Script!" Swings the nightstick like he's performing an exorcism. Silas sacrificed him once under Protocol Zero. He came back anyway.

### Fighting style
Agile striker. Fast hands, high volume. (AshLane: `boxing`, archetype `striker`)

## 5. KIKO TANAKA

**Role:** "The Detective" — stealth specialist, Level 4
**Based on:** The Great Muta (Keiji Muto)
**Fighting style reference:** Lei Wulong (Tekken) — Hong Kong police detective

### Picture references (for artists/modelers)
- Great Muta: search "Great Muta face paint" (green/red mist era)
- Lei Wulong: Tekken 7/8 character art — trench coat detective
- Tekken Wiki: https://tekken.fandom.com/wiki/Lei_Wulong

### Physical reference (Great Muta)
- Japanese man, ~6'2", ~230 lb
- Face paint tradition (Muta's iconic painted face — adapt as tactical face paint, not full Muta paint)
- Long dark hair (can be tied back)
- Athletic build

### Fighting style reference (Lei Wulong)
- **Five Animals kung fu:** Dragon, Snake, Tiger, Leopard, Crane stances
- **Drunken fist (Zuiquan)** — unpredictable, flowing
- Hong Kong police detective — trench coat, dedicated "supercop"
- Based on Jackie Chan's Police Story energy

### AshLane uniform
- **Detective trench coat** — dark (navy/black), worn open
- Plainclothes underneath (dark shirt, tactical pants)
- **Night-vision goggles** — pushed up on forehead when not in use
- Shoulder holster (detective-style, not duty belt)
- Gloves (tactical, fingerless)
- Boots (quiet soles — he's stealth)
- Optional: subtle face paint markings (Muta nod, tactical not theatrical)

### Signature moves (from canon + owner)
- **The Shining Wizard** (Muta's iconic knee strike — owner confirmed)
- **The Phantom Lock** (submission — "by the time you see him, it's already on")
- **The Shadow Slip** (rapid dodges — "makes him seem invisible")
- Stance-switching between animal forms (Lei Wulong parallel)

### Vibe
The Detective. Plainclothes, always watching. Works alone. Files reports nobody reads. You never see him coming. Switches fighting stances mid-combo like he's changing personalities.

### Fighting style
Speedster/martial artist. Stance-switching, unpredictable. (AshLane: `martial-arts`, archetype `speedster`)

## 7. ASTRID RESKIN PLAN

**Source model:** `wwe_rhea_ripley_2k22` (from Bannon repo)
**Target:** Astrid "The Ice Maiden" in Dynasty Authority tactical gear

### What stays the same
- Face geometry and likeness (DO NOT remodel)
- Body proportions
- Hair geometry (long black — can stay)
- Tattoos (keep or subtly adapt)

### What changes (texture repaint)
1. **Torso:** Dark navy tactical vest / plate carrier with "POLICE" chest marking
2. **Arms:** Dark long-sleeve tactical shirt (navy/black)
3. **Legs:** Dark cargo pants with knee pads
4. **Belt:** Duty belt — cuffs, radio, holster (painted detail)
5. **Boots:** Black tactical boots
6. **Badge:** Small chest badge (painted or decal)
7. **Skin:** Keep natural skin tone on face/neck/hands

### Technical approach
- Procedural texture repaint (same pipeline as Sombra Negra reskin: `~/workspace/sombra-negra/`)
- UV-mapped texture edit — no geometry changes
- Output: `ASTRID_authority.glb` in `public/models/cast/`
- Roster entry: `astrid_authority`

### Legal note
Rhea Ripley model is a WWE game rip (like all `wwe2k22_*` meshes). Reskin does NOT clear copyright. Per owner's reskin policy: lightly reskinned to match the likeness of the canon character (Astrid). Commercial release requires remodel/replacement.

## 8. FACTION UNIFORM SYSTEM

### Shared elements (all 6 members)
- Police blue family: navy `#1a2f5a`, dark blue `#243b6b`, tactical blue `#2e4a7a`
- Badge steel accent: `#b8c4d4`
- "POLICE" or "DYNASTY AUTHORITY" markings
- Duty belt or tactical belt (everyone)

### Individual variations
| Member | Base | Signature piece | Color note |
|--------|------|-----------------|------------|
| Silas | White shirt + slacks | Open collar, rolled sleeves, gun belt | White/navy (command) |
| Titus | Riot gear | Full plates, no helmet | Black (breacher) |
| Great White North | Tactical carrier | Chain on fist, shield option | Dark blue |
| Finn Mac | Clerical black | Priest collar, nightstick-as-cross | Black (chaplain) |
| Kiko Tanaka | Trench coat | NVG goggles, plainclothes | Navy/black (detective) |
| Astrid | Tactical vest | Severe hair, joint-targeting | Dark navy (warden) |

### Grunt uniforms (generated)
- Beat cops: standard blue uniform, cap or bare head
- SWAT: black tactical, helmet (grunts CAN wear helmets — only Titus goes without)
- Detectives: plainclothes + badge on belt
- All use the `authority` faction palette in char-gen.ts
