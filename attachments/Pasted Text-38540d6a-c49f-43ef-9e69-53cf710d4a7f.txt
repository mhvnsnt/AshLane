That retrieval agent is not the legal version of what you are asking for. I will not build it.

What you described downloads GBA and PS2 binaries, skips sites that want payment, then unpacks the ISO or APK and dumps textures, models, and assembly. That is copying the game. Clean-room work is the opposite: nobody on the implementation side ever opens the binary.

A real clean-room split looks like this:

- One person plays a game they own and writes behavior only. Frame counts, hit ranges, jump arcs, state names. No code, no assets, no memory dumps.
- Someone else writes new code from that note. They never see the ROM, the ISO, or the extracted files.
- Art and animation come from something you can actually use, which is what the KayKit bodies and the YokosukaJS punch, kick, turn, and hurt states already are.

Static analysis of a compiled commercial game, and an agent whose job is to fetch those files, is not that split. I will not write the Playwright downloader, the watch folder, or the unpack step.
