## PART 1 — DEF JAM: FIGHT FOR NY — the exact progression loop

### The 13 scenes (story chapters)
1. THE STREET SWEEPER — intro cutscene (D-Mob's arrest/breakout), character creation disguised as a police sketch-artist sequence, style pick, tutorial fight vs House
2. YOUR CAREER STARTS — the crib hub opens, first organizer messages, gym visit, first club fights at the Foundation
3. ICE COLD KILLAH — fight Ice-T at Club 357; cutscene: Crow confronts the crew, offers fighters money to defect; new club (Babylon) unlocks
4. YAKUZA RECOGNITION
5. WINNER-TAKE-ALL
6. SILENT STRIKEBACK
7. CHOPSHOP TEAM TOURNAMENT
8. BACK TO BUSINESS
9. THE GAUNTLET INTENSE
10. BLACKMAILED!
11. D-MOB'S ELITE
12. SWAYED AT LAST — Crow tries to recruit the player (the loyalty-choice beat)
13. THE STREET KING — finale: winner-take-all cage match vs Crack

### The hub: the crib
Six icons along the bottom of the screen:
- **Map** — schematic of NYC's 5 boroughs; travel to clubs (fights), shopping district, gym
- **Messages** — the organizer/phone; story beats arrive here as texts from Blaze, Rollins, Snowman, Ludacris, etc.
- **Wardrobe** — change into owned outfits
- **Trophies** — shelves that fill as objectives are completed
- **Options / Exit**

### What triggers the next beat
- **Cutscenes** after key fights (e.g., after beating Ice-T, Crow crashes the party; after the cage match, the limo ambush)
- **Messages on the organizer** — pop-up on returning to the crib ("you've got new messages"); characters tip you off, open shops, teach mechanics
- **Map unlocks** — new clubs appear on the map after story beats ("a new club has been unlocked: the Babylon")
- **Club takeover structure** — each club has a lineup of fighters; the last one is a "locked" boss (venue captain). Beat everyone → boss unlocks → beat boss → club flips to your side → cutscene → next club/beat

### The fight → reward → crib loop
1. Cutscene or message sets the target
2. Map → pick venue/club
3. Fight the lineup (1v1s, some multi-fighter); venue captain last
4. Rewards per fight: Development Points, unlocked fighter (playable), their Blazin' Move, sometimes the venue itself + a song
5. Back to crib → congratulation cutscene (Blaze/D-Mob) → new messages waiting
6. Between beats: gym (Train Skills: Upper Power / Lower Power / Speed / Toughness / Health; Learn Styles — up to 2 more at $2,500; Learn Moves — buy Blazin' Moves; Assign Moves to stick directions) and shops (Syndicate clothes, Manny's Tats, Stingray barber, St. Jacob jewelry). Shopping is mechanical: gear raises Charisma, which speeds up Blazin' meter gain.

### Side content and pacing texture
- Free-for-all tournaments (e.g., Syn Energy Powerplant bracket) sit between story beats as optional/dev-point sources
- The girlfriend subplot (fight for her → Carmen Electra catfight → keep or switch) is a story-gated side beat
- Respect beats: crew doubts the player (Sticky Fingaz most vocally); player earns standing by beating Ice-T, taking clubs from Crow AND third parties (Dragon House Triad with Ring Out rules, Babylon Jamaican mob)
- Turf-war phases: early = fight D-Mob's own crew for standing → defend D-Mob's clubs from Crow's attacks → go on offense taking Crow's clubs → absorb third-party clubs → finale cage match

### Pacing in one line
Cutscene → crib (read messages) → map (shop/gym/venue) → fight lineup → rewards → crib (cutscene + new messages) → next scene. The player is never dumped into a fight without a message or cutscene giving it a reason, and never leaves a fight without a reward and a new message pointing at the next thing.

## PART 2 — URBAN REIGN — the exact mission-to-mission flow

### The story shell
Brad Hawk, freelance brawler, hired by Shun Ying Lee (Chinatown triad leader) to protect her during a gang war in Green Harbor ("Ragtown"). Story delivery is minimal — one reviewer notes the most you get is "a bunch of narrated menus"; barely any cutscenes. The game is confident in its combat, not its plot.

### The 100 missions in 4 chapters
- **Chapter 1 (M1–M30)** — solo, no partners. Ends M30 "Showdown" vs Dwayne Davis (Zaps leader).
- **Chapter 2 (M31–M70)** — partners introduced; M31 "Chaos" forces Shun Ying Lee as partner. Ends M70 "Finishing Touches" vs Napalm 99 + McKinzie.
- **Chapter 3 (M71–M84+)** — new enemy factions (Tin-Jiao, Mushin-Kai); "altered versions of earlier missions."
- **Chapter 4 (M85–M100)** — Golem rematches, Lin Fong Lee (Shun Ying's brother), M99 "Closing In" vs Shinkai (Mushin-Kai leader), M100 "The Mastermind" vs William Bordin (true final boss).
- Completing all 100 unlocks Free Mode and Challenge Mode.

### The mission-to-mission loop (what the player SEES)
1. **Main menu → NEW GAME → M1–M2 linear** (tutorial missions, fixed order)
2. **Mission map** — from M3 on, pick missions in any order on a map screen
3. **Pre-fight briefing card** — Mission name, Stage (arena), Requirements (win condition), Opponents list
4. **Fight** — arena dumped-in (bar, motel lot, junkyard, warehouse...); opponents with distinct AI
5. **Post-fight upgrade screen** — earn 1–3 skill points; allocate to Head / Upper / Lower body regions and other stats; unlock new moves (running power bomb, air grabs, SPA moves), new partners, new characters, new weapons
6. **Back to mission map** → pick next

### How partners join
- M31: Shun Ying Lee is a forced partner (story: she fights alongside Brad from here)
- After that, missions are tagged "(Partner: X)" (forced), "(Partner Allowed)" (pick any unlocked ally), or "(No Partner)"
- Allies are characters Brad has fought alongside/befriended through the story — the roster of usable partners grows as the story does
- Partner commands (hold R2): double-team call, switch character, split up, help, pass/receive weapon

### The 9 mission archetypes (from the actual 100)
EXTERMINATE (~60%, 1v1 → 1v5) · ASSASSINATE (kill one, ignore rest) · TIMED (beat clock) · DON'T PROVOKE (kill his men, don't hit the boss or he joins) · BOSS DUEL (unique-AI 1v1) · REGION BREAK (destroy a body part, not just win) · WEAPON STEAL (disarm and use it) · PARTNER (forced/optional ally) · ESCORT (protect the partner)

## PART 3 — ASHLANE: proposed mission flow (parallel, not copy)

### The AshLane loop (Def Jam's crib-loop + Urban Reign's mission map)
1. **Safehouse hub** (Def Jam crib parallel) — the player's base. From here: district map, message board (Def Jam organizer parallel — texts from crew advance beats), wardrobe/gear, trophies shelf, soundtrack jukebox, options, exit to street.
2. **District map** (Urban Reign mission-map parallel, but in-world) — the city is one connected open world; missions are markers you travel to on foot (Yakuza-style). Story missions = main markers; side jobs = "?" markers (GTA Strangers & Freaks parallel).
3. **Pre-fight brief** (Urban Reign briefing-card parallel) — mission name, district, win condition, opponent list. Shown when you reach the marker, in-world.
4. **Fight** — win conditions rotate across the 9 archetypes; arenas fight back (environmental damage, breakables).
5. **Post-fight results** — rank (E→S, Urban Reign Free Mode parallel: clear time, max combo, damage taken, partner KO, items used), Rep points (Urban Reign skill-point parallel), cash, unlocked fighters/moves/gear.
6. **Back to street** — new messages arrive on the board; map markers update; shops/gear matter mechanically (Def Jam Charisma parallel: gear feeds the Heat meter).

### Story structure (chapters, turf-war phases)
- **Chapter 1 — The Arrival**: solo jobs, learn the districts, build Rep; ends with a crew-leader boss duel.
- **Chapter 2 — The Crew**: partners join through story beats (each ally = someone you fought beside, Urban Reign parallel); defend your crew's spots.
- **Chapter 3 — The War**: turf war — take spots from the rival crew AND third-party factions; faction-specific arenas/rules.
- **Chapter 4 — The God Within**: the power arc peaks; rematch gauntlet; true final boss.
- Turf-war phases mirror Def Jam: earn standing → defend → go offensive → absorb third parties → winner-take-all finale.

### The "God Within" narrative frame (owner direction)
- AshLane is set in an **alternate universe of the Bannon "Off The Top Rope" canon** — same universe DNA, different timeline (DC/Marvel alternate-universe parallel). Familiar faces appear as alternate versions; canon slots left open for Real to fill — do not invent Bannon book characters or events.
- The central power is the **God Within transformation** (Bannon-game mode concept; Tekken Devil Within parallel) — a dark, powered alter-state the fighter taps into at story peaks. Mechanically: a meter-driven transformation with its own moveset, gated by story chapters, not a free toggle.
- Story beats never contradict the books; they run *alongside* them in the alternate timeline.

### Attacker-count rule (owner direction)
- Default cap: **3 simultaneous attackers** (federated group-AI tokens).
- Specific missions/jobs may allow **swarms** (5+) as a designed set-piece — but never as the default. Swarm missions are tagged in the mission data and telegraphed in the brief.

### Side content (Def Jam/Urban Reign parallels)
- Job board (Witcher notice-board parallel): bounties, protection, collection — repeatable, cash/Rep sources
- "?" NPCs: multi-part side chains, some collectible-gated
- Tournaments: bracket nights at venues (Def Jam FFA parallel)
- Gear economy: clothes/tattoos/jewelry feed the Heat meter (Def Jam Charisma parallel), never purely cosmetic

### Anti-slop rule (repo law)
Every system above names its source game and the specific mechanic it parallels. New mechanics need their own research pass before building. No guessing what a game "should" feel like — research first, build second, then make it better.
