## URBAN REIGN (PS2, 2005 — Namco)

### Game Flow (exact)
```
MAIN MENU
├── NEW GAME → Story Mode (play as Brad Hawk)
├── CONTINUE → Resume save
├── FREE → Replay story missions with any unlocked character (unlocks after beating story)
├── MULTIPLAYER → Up to 4 players
├── EXTRAS
│   ├── TUTORIAL
│   ├── PRACTICE (unlimited health/time)
│   ├── FIGHTER FILES (profiles of unlocked characters)
│   └── CREDITS
└── OPTIONS (Game/Camera, Controller, Display, Sound, Difficulty 5 levels, Autosave)
```

**Story Mode flow:**
1. Missions 1-2: Linear tutorial (forced order)
2. After Mission 2: **Mission select MAP** — choose missions in any order
3. After EACH mission: **Upgrade screen** — spend 1-3 earned points on Brad Hawk's stats
4. Certain missions unlock: new moves for Brad, new characters, new weapons for multiplayer
5. 100 missions total, organized in chapters (Chapter 1 = Missions 1-30)

### Complete Mission Archetype Breakdown

**Type 1: EXTERMINATE** — "Take everyone down" (most common, ~60% of missions)
- *Mission 1: Warming Up* — Back Alley, vs. Em Cee + Real Deal (2 opponents, tutorial)
- *Mission 17: Crashing the Party* — Southend, vs. 5 opponents (Busta, Em Cee, Miguel, Spider, Ty)
- *Mission 25: Outnumbered* — vs. 5 opponents (the other 5v1)
- Enemy counts scale: 1v1 → 1v2 → 1v3 → 1v4 → 1v5

**Type 2: ASSASSINATE** — "Take [specific target] down" (ignore others)
- *Mission 3: Declaration* — Bar, vs. Busta only (1v1)
- *Mission 4: Friendly Gesture* — Hideout, vs. Rod/Seth/Torque — "Take ONE of them down" (pick weakest: Rod)
- *Mission 14: Right Stuff* — Bar, vs. Glen + Jake — "Take Glen down" (DON'T hit Jake or he joins)
- *Mission 34: The Agitator* — vs. Bain + 3 Zaps — "Take Bain down" (avoid others, go straight for target)

**Type 3: TIMED** — "Take X down within the Time Limit"
- *Mission 6: Turf* — Motel, vs. Reggie Boone, **2:00 limit**. Music gets louder as time runs out. Timer on top of screen.
- *Mission 18: Hit-and-Run* — Bar, vs. Rod + Torque, time limit. Use environmental grapples (throw into tables) for speed.
- *Mission 38: The Derelict* — vs. 3 opponents, time limit.

**Type 4: DON'T PROVOKE** — "Take X's men down, DON'T hit [boss]"
- *Mission 10: Observer* — Motel, vs. Glen + 3 men. "Take Glen's men down." If you hit Glen, he joins the fight. Music changes when he activates.
- *Mission 14: Right Stuff* — Same concept with Jake as the "don't touch" boss.

**Type 5: BOSS DUEL** — 1v1 vs. major character with unique AI
- *Mission 11: The Proof* — Bar, vs. Jake Hudson (wrestler, uses grabs, counters combos, brutal SPAs)
- *Mission 15: Fury* — vs. Kadonashi (has "Charge SPA" — glows green, longer combos, more damage)
- *Mission 20: The Hungry Wolf* — Warehouse, vs. Tong Yoon (kickboxer, high lower-body endurance — "DON'T USE LOWER BODY GRAPPLES")
- *Mission 30: Showdown* — vs. Dwayne Davis (end of Chapter 1, deflects heavily)

**Type 6: REGION BREAK** — "Bust up [specific body region]"
- *Mission 23: Avenging the Honor* — vs. Zach Parker. Objective: **"Bust up Zach's lower body"** (not just defeat — destroy a specific region). Use sweep kicks (Down+Circle) + lower grapples.

**Type 7: WEAPON STEAL** — Opponent has weapon, disarm and use it
- *Mission 28: Who's Your Daddy?* — vs. Glen Kluger wielding a **crowbar**. "Use your Up SPA to knock the crowbar out of his hand, then pick it up and use it."

**Type 8: PARTNER MISSIONS** (start at Mission 31)
- *Forced partner*: "Partner: Shun Ying Lee" (Mission 31), "Partner: Glen Kluger" (Mission 36), "Partner: Dwayne Davis" (Mission 40)
- *Optional partner*: "Partner Allowed" (Missions 32-35, 38-39, 43-45...) — choose your own

**Type 9: ESCORT / PROTECT** — "Take enemy down while protecting [partner]"
- *Mission 36: Despicable Ally* — Partner: Glen Kluger. Protect him while fighting BK, Booma, Bain.
- *Mission 40: Dead Run* — Partner: Dwayne Davis. "You start at the other end of the street while Dwayne is already brawling. Run to him and dash-attack enemies off him."
- *Mission 41: Traitor* — Partner: Grimm. Protect him vs. 4 boxers.
- *Mission 42: Lucky Error* — Partner: Tong Yoon. Protect him.

### Character Select / Fighter Files
- 60+ characters, each with: fighting style, stats, movelist, unlock method
- Styles include: Wrestling, Kickboxing, Boxing, Capoeira (Chris Bowman — "like Eddy/Christie from Tekken"), Karate, etc.
- Tekken characters Paul Phoenix and Marshall Law are unlockable

### Upgrade System (exact)
- After each mission: earn **1-3 stat points**
- Spend on Brad Hawk's abilities (player chooses allocation)
- Also unlock: new moves for Brad, new playable characters, new multiplayer weapons
- "How you strengthen your abilities is up to you. You will be allowed to edit at the end of each level."

### Partner System (exact controls)
Hold **R2** + button:
- **R2 alone**: Double-Team attack call
- **R2 + X**: Switch character/partner (swap who you control)
- **R2 + Circle**: Split up (partner goes solo)
- **R2 + Triangle**: Help (partner comes to you)
- **R2 + Square**: Pass Weapon / Receive Weapon

**Double-team attacks** (context-sensitive Triangle):
- Partner in front + you in front of opponent → Triangle = front double-team
- You behind + partner in front → Triangle = sandwich double-team
- Both behind → Triangle = rear double-team

### Body Region System (exact)
Every character has 3 regions: **Head / Upper / Lower**
- Each region has independent health with 3 states:
  - **Green**: Fine
  - **Yellow + blue lightning**: Injured (weaker)
  - **Red + red lightning**: Destroyed (very vulnerable)
- Shown on: small body icon next to health/SPA meter
- Also shown via lightning effects on the body part when hit
- Characters have different strengths/weaknesses per region (e.g., Tong Yoon has max lower-body endurance)
- Moves target specific regions — "knowing a character's strength and weakness in regions can be the difference between victory and defeat"

### Combat Mechanics That Matter
- **Juggle system** (Tekken-style): launch → air combo. Enemies can dodge mid-juggle with precise timing.
- **Dodge/Deflect**: Single button, must be timed to attack. Up/Down + dodge = reversal.
- **Special Arts (SPA)**: Two-button press, can't be countered except by another SPA. Meter must be at threshold. Can be buffered.
- **Charge SPA**: Some bosses (Kadonashi) power up — glow green, longer combos, more damage. Temporary.
- **Environmental damage**: Throw into tables, chairs, walls, shelves for bonus damage. Bar and Warehouse stages emphasize this.
- **Health drinks**: Defeated enemies drop them. Pick up with L1, drink with Circle. One-time use. Discard with L1.
- **Weapons**: Pick up with L1. Can be knocked out of hands with Up SPA.

### Stages (confirmed from missions)
Back Alley, Chinatown, Bar, Hideout, Southend, Motel, Warehouse, Parking Lot, Subway...

## DEF JAM: FIGHT FOR NY (2004 — EA Chicago)

### Story Structure (exact)
1. **Character creation** disguised as police sketch: After D-Mob's car crash, cops ask witness (you) to describe the suspect to sketch artist Lauren. "What did his hair look like?" "What about his nose?" = choosing your appearance. 5 voice options (PS2/Xbox).
2. **Crib hub** containing:
   - **T-Mobile Sidekick**: Check messages (story progression)
   - **Trophy wall**: Notable accomplishments
   - **Map of 5 NYC boroughs**: Select venues/fights
   - **Shops** (Staten Island shopping district)
3. **Turf war**: Clubs change control when you beat the captain or clear all fighters. Fight for D-Mob → defend from Crow → take Crow's clubs → take third-party clubs (Dragon House, Babylon).
4. Winning unlocks: defeated fighters (playable in Battle Mode), their Blazin' Move, their jewelry.

### Shops (exact)
- **Clothes**: Reebok, Phat Farm, Air Jordan, Sean John (Diddy), and more. Can adjust HOW worn: hoody up/down, hat backwards/tilted, pants sagging.
- **Jewelry**: Jacob "The Jeweler" Arabo. Unlock defeated fighters' jewelry (Sean Paul, Fat Joe, Xzibit, etc.)
- **Tattoos**: Manny
- **Haircuts**: Stingray
- **Gym** (Henry Rollins): Spend Development Points on:
  - Stats: speed, strength, toughness, etc.
  - New Blazin' Moves
  - Up to 2 ADDITIONAL fighting styles (start with 1, can have 3 total)

### 5 Fighting Styles (mechanical differences)
Each style has a **unique KO method** — you CANNOT win by just depleting health. When opponent is in "danger" (flashing red), you must use:
1. **Street Fighting** → Strong punch (haymaker)
2. **Wrestling** → Strong grapple (smackdown)
3. **Kickboxing** → Kick-combo finisher
4. **Martial Arts** → Flying attack (must launch off wall)
5. **Submission** → Force submission (repeatedly bend a body part until it gives)

You can combine up to 3 styles, giving more KO options.

### Health System (unique — not just a bar)
- **TWO bars**: Consciousness (opaque light green) + Physical Wellness (semi-transparent dark green)
- Consciousness drops faster than wellness with each hit
- Consciousness **recovers** up to the wellness cap when not taking damage
- **Danger state**: Consciousness very low → entire bar turns RED → must land a KO move NOW
- **Permanent danger**: Wellness so low that consciousness can't recover to green — permanently vulnerable

### Momentum / Blazin' System
- Gain momentum by: landing moves, countering, taunting
- **Charisma stat** affects momentum gain rate
- Charisma boosted by: expensive clothes, tattoos, jewelry ("a fighter laden with jewelry can fill momentum in just a few moves")
- Full momentum → activate **Blazin'** (taunt animation) → can use **Blazin' Move**
- Created fighter can learn ALL Blazin' Moves but equip only 4 at a time
- Blazin' Moves are personalized brutal finishers (like Fatalities)

### All Match Types (with venues and rules)
1. **1 on 1** — Standard
2. **2 on 2** — Ends ONLY when both fighters on a team are KO'd/submitted
3. **Free for All** — 3-4 fighters, every fighter for themselves
4. **Cage Match** — No crowd interference. Cage itself is a weapon. *Venue: Club Murder*
5. **Ring Out Match** — Wooden barriers break. Win by throwing opponent through gap OR by KO. *Venue: Dragon House* (Triad-run)
6. **Inferno Match** — Burning building. Ring of fire damages on contact. Flaming debris falls from ceiling as weapons. *Venue: Red Hook Tire Co.*
7. **Demolition Match** — Two SUVs in arena (P1: black Escalade, P2: silver Hummer). Win by totaling opponent's car: smash them into 2 different car sections 3 times. *Venue: Gun Hill Garage*
8. **Subway Match** — Train passes at intervals. Throw/knock opponent into train path = **instant win regardless of health**. Double KO if both hit. *Venue: 125th Street Station*
9. **Window Match** — 3 large windows, 4-story drop. Smash through any = win. *Venue: Crow's Office*

**Rule variants** (any match type):
- Standard: No holds barred
- Favorite: Point-based judging (winner = highest style points, not necessarily who got KO'd)
- Submissions: No submission holds allowed

### Crowd System (exact)
- "Tightly packed cheering crowd"
- **Hands weapons** to fighters (pipes, bottles, bats, shovels, bricks, pliers, sledgehammers)
- **Holds fighters** who get too close — leaves them open for cheap shots
- **Shoves** fighters back into combat
- Spectators will **attack** a fighter held by a nearby onlooker

### Environmental Combat
- Slam into walls headfirst for massive damage
- Ram doors/gates in faces
- Columns, parked SUVs cause damage and **show visible damage**
- Fighters develop **cuts and bruises** during fight
- Clothes and jewelry **flap/react** to movement

## DEF JAM: ICON (2007 — EA Chicago)

**Core innovation: Music IS the combat system**
- Right analog stick = turntable. Scratch to:
  - Switch songs (damage bonus when YOUR song plays)
  - Rewind song → **force entire background to explode**
- Stages pulse to the beat. Hazards trigger on beat (every ~4 bars).
- Strategy: knock opponent into danger zone → scratch to detonate.
- Hazards: exploding gas pumps, swinging cars, bass-wave speakers, helicopter tails, falling lights, pole dancers kicking.
- Custom soundtracks supported — beat-matching engine adapts to any song.
- Removed from FFNY: Blazin' Moves, style customization, weapons (replaced by beat system).

## DEF JAM: VENDETTA (2003 — EA/AKI)
- Predecessor to FFNY. Primarily a wrestling game (AKI engine — same as WWF No Mercy).
- 4 pre-set characters (no creation).
- Foundation that FFNY expanded with styles, Blazin', and street elements.

## What Makes Them Feel Like "Real Games" (the anti-bullshit list)

### 1. Mission/Objective Variety
Urban Reign has 9 distinct mission types, not "fight guys" 100 times. Def Jam has 9 match types with different win conditions. **AshLane needs**: More than one way to win.

### 2. Progression That Matters
- Urban Reign: 1-3 points per mission, allocate to stats, unlock moves/characters/weapons
- Def Jam: Cash → clothes (charisma → momentum), stats, new styles, Blazin' Moves
- **AshLane needs**: A reason to play mission 2 after mission 1 beyond "another fight"

### 3. A Hub That Feels Like a Place
- Def Jam's crib: messages, trophies, map, shops — you LIVE there
- Urban Reign's mission map: choose your path
- **AshLane needs**: Not a menu list. A space.

### 4. Combat With Decisions
- Urban Reign: Target weak regions, manage SPA meter, use environment, command partner
- Def Jam: Style-specific KOs, two-bar health mindgames, momentum/charisma economy, crowd as weapon
- **AshLane needs**: Fighting that's about more than mashing

### 5. The World Fights Back
- Def Jam: Crowd holds you, trains kill you, windows drop you 4 stories
- Urban Reign: Health drinks, weapons, environmental damage, partner AI
- **AshLane needs**: Stages that aren't just backgrounds

### 6. Character Identity
- Urban Reign: 60+ characters with distinct styles, stats, regions
- Def Jam: Created fighter with your clothes, your jewelry, your styles, your Blazin' Moves
- **AshLane needs**: Fighters that feel different, not reskins
