# The Dynasty Authority — Police Faction Bible

> **AshLane street version of Bannon Book 4's Dynasty Authority.**
> They're the LAW in AshLane — but a twisted version. Not heroes.

## Canon Source

From `canon/04_book4_core_unlocked.md` (Bannon Book 4):

> The Dynasty Authority — a "sanctioned" police faction made of former wrestlers
> (Captain Silas plus five officers) enforcing arbitrary "laws" (Peace Act,
> "Resisting the narrative," "Resisting arrest") via brutal force; explicitly
> criminalizes both excess violence AND pacifism — they want endless conflict
> to justify their own existence as authority. Dresses first in SWAT gear, then
> (per author correction) in traditional blue police uniforms.

In Book 4, they issued a **Class-A warrant** for "Marquis Deshaun Whitacre,
alias Bannon, alias Maime." That warrant was never formally resolved or
rescinded. The survivors (Silas, Astrid, Kiko Tanaka, Great White North) were
defeated but not confirmed dead.

## AshLane Street Version

In Earth-AL, the Dynasty Authority is the city's police department — staffed
by former fighters who couldn't leave the violence behind. They wear tactical
police gear (SWAT blacks for raids, traditional blues for patrol — per the
author's correction, both are canon).

They enforce "order" through violence. The laws they enforce are arbitrary:

- **The Peace Act** — any unsanctioned gathering of 3+ people is an unlawful assembly
- **Resisting the Narrative** — contradicting an officer's account is a crime
- **Resisting Arrest** — running from a cop who hasn't told you you're under arrest is still resisting
- **Protocol Zero** — an officer may be sacrificed to complete an objective (Silas used this on Finn Mac)

Their core philosophy: they criminalize BOTH excess violence AND pacifism.
They need endless low-level conflict to justify their own existence as
authority. A quiet street means budget cuts. So the street is never quiet.

### Distinct from Kennedy's Corporate Structure

The Dynasty Authority are **cops**. Kennedy's people are **corporate security**.
Different uniforms, different bosses, different paperwork — but on the street,
the difference is academic. They don't coordinate. They sometimes compete over
jurisdiction. A Combine operative and an Authority officer will argue over who
gets to beat you up first.

## The Six Officers

### Captain Silas "The System" — Commissioner

- **Canon:** LP 4/Exp 8. Cold, bureaucratic, treats people as "variables" to be
  processed. "Data Wrestler" gimmick — black bodysuit with glowing blue digital
  circuitry, reviews pre-match data on a tablet. Technical Analyst style.
  Finishers: **The Algorithm** (Double-Arm Underhook DDT to Sleeper Hold),
  **The Optimization Drive** (Wrist-clutch Exploder Suplex).
- **AshLane street persona:** KEEP. He's the Commissioner. Runs the precinct
  like a data center — crime stats on wall screens, patrol routes optimized by
  algorithm. Still reviews "data" on a tablet before a raid. Cold, detached,
  never raises his voice. Treats citizens as variables.
- **Role:** Faction boss. Final police encounter.
- **Fighting style:** Technical (martial-arts) — patient, reads you, punishes patterns.
- **Archetype:** Balanced. **Quirk:** By The Book.

### "Big Dawg" Titus — S.W.A.T. / Muscle Enforcer

- **Canon:** Police Faction S.W.A.T./muscle enforcer. Eliminated twice — once by
  Finxsse's Northern Lights Bomb, once thrown off the Tower roof by Iida. Iida
  later beat Officer Titus with a Kinshasa, debuting "BIYATCH!"
- **AshLane street persona:** KEEP. SWAT breacher. First through the door,
  last one standing. Big, loud, loves the gear. The battering ram.
- **Role:** Heavy enforcer. Breach-and-clear specialist.
- **Fighting style:** Powerhouse brawler (street). **Archetype:** Bruiser.
- **Quirk:** Brawler.

### The Great White North — "The Jailer"

- **Canon:** LP 8/Exp 7. Canadian. Powerhouse/Brawler. Carries chains and
  manacles as the faction's "Jailer." Finisher: **The Frostbite Driver**
  (Delayed Brainbuster). Eliminated by Shinobi (low blow + Killswitch), then
  pinned by Stick-Up.
- **AshLane street persona:** KEEP. The transport officer. Carries zip-ties and
  a chain wrapped around his fist. Decides who gets "processed" and who gets
  "handled on-site." Quiet. The chains aren't for show.
- **Role:** Grappler. Arrests you — permanently.
- **Fighting style:** Grappler (wrestling). **Archetype:** Tank. **Quirk:** Loyal.

### Finn "The Priest" Mac — "The Chaplain"

- **Canon:** LP 7/Exp 11. Police Faction "Chaplain" — wields a nightstick "like
  a cross." Religious-fanatic dialogue: *"Confess! Resistance is a sin against
  the Script!"* Engaged in a "Holy War" against Stick-Up (dueling messianic
  deliriums). Sacrificed by Silas's "Protocol Zero" to cripple Stick-Up's knee.
  Finisher: **The Confession** (Twisting Brainbuster).
- **AshLane street persona:** KEEP. The precinct chaplain who never left the
  beat. Swings the nightstick like a crucifix. Demands confessions mid-fight.
  Genuinely believes the badge is divine mandate.
- **Role:** Striker. Zealot with a baton.
- **Fighting style:** Striker (boxing). **Archetype:** Striker. **Quirk:** Confess.

### Kiko Tanaka — "The Detective"

- **Canon:** "The Ghost." Psychological/Aerial. Night-vision goggles, stealth
  specialist. Signatures: The Shadow Slip (rapid dodges), Shining Wizard,
  Dragon Screw, The Spectral Drop (standing Moonsault). Finishers:
  **The Phantom Lock** (sleeper from counter-position), **The Black Fog**
  (blinding mist + pin). Struck in the throat by Magdalena's staff.
- **AshLane street persona:** KEEP. Plainclothes detective. Night-vision
  goggles on the forehead, always watching. You never see him coming — the
  Shadow Slip is real. Works alone. Files reports nobody reads.
- **Role:** Speedster. Infiltration and ambush.
- **Fighting style:** Speedster (martial-arts). **Archetype:** Speedster.
- **Quirk:** Opportunist.

### Astrid "The Ice Maiden" — "The Warden"

- **Canon:** LP 4/Exp 8. Swedish. Cold Structuralist — emotionless, precise.
  "Warden" — cold biomechanical striker targeting joints. Finisher:
  **The Scandinavian Lock** (Sharpshooter). Submitted to Maime's Rings of Saturn.
- **AshLane street persona:** KEEP. Runs the holding cells. Cold, clinical —
  targets joints because "a broken wrist can't hold a weapon." Never raises her
  voice. The most feared officer on the force, including by other officers.
- **Role:** Technical striker. Joint destruction.
- **Fighting style:** Technical striker (muay-thai — clinch, elbows, joint attacks).
- **Archetype:** Striker. **Quirk:** Counter.

## Faction Relationships

- **vs The Ashes:** The street vs the law. Ashes see Authority as occupiers. Authority sees Ashes as "unlawful assembly."
- **vs The Combine (Kennedy Corp):** Jurisdiction rivalry. Cops vs corporate security. They don't coordinate — they compete over who gets to crack down first.
- **vs The Hollows:** Authority writes them off as vagrants. Hollows laugh at the uniforms.
- **vs The Painted:** Authority considers the carnival grounds a "public nuisance." The Painted consider cops "the best audience."
- **vs Unaffiliated:** Mercenaries sometimes take Authority contracts. Sometimes take contracts against them. Business is business.
