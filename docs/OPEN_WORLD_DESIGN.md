# AshLane Open World Design (2026-10-05)
Yakuza-style: dense, connected, travel to missions.

## World Structure
Not huge empty — dense like Kamurocho. Districts connected by streets.

### Districts (all connected, walkable)
1. **The Strip** — main commercial street. Shops, NPCs, mission hub.
2. **Back Alleys** — narrow, ambush missions. Urban Reign-style.
3. **Warehouse District** — industrial. Boss fights, weapon missions.
4. **Park** — grass, trees, animals (birds, stray dogs). Calm before storm.
5. **Subway** — underground. Subway match type (Def Jam).
6. **Rooftops** — connected via fire escapes. Window/ring-out missions.

### How Missions Work (NOT isolated levels)
- You START in the open world (wherever you were).
- Mission markers appear on map (GTA-style letters, Yakuza-style icons).
- Walk/drive to the marker. Talk to NPC or walk into trigger.
- The fight happens WHERE YOU ARE — the world doesn't unload.
- After mission, you're still in the world. Walk to the next one.

### NPC Interactions
- **Talk** (Yakuza): Walk up, press button. Get side missions, tips, shop access.
- **Shopkeepers**: Buy clothes (Charisma/Heat), weapons, health items.
- **Pedestrians**: Walk around, react to fights (run away, watch, call cops?).
- **Rival gang members**: Roam. Attack on sight or when provoked.

### Travel
- **Walk**: Default. Dense enough that walking works.
- **Fast travel**: Taxi stands (Yakuza) or subway stations. Must discover first.
- **No loading screens** between districts (streaming).

### Side Content (Yakuza-style)
- **Side stories**: NPC with icon overhead. Walk up to trigger.
- **Minigames**: Pool hall, arcade, etc. (find open-source templates).
- **Collectibles**: Hidden packages, etc.

### Day/Night
- Time passes. Different enemies at night. Shops have hours.
- Park has animals during day, thugs at night.
